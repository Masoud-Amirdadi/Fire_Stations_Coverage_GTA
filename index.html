02 / THREE LENSES, ONE SYSTEM

Where? How far?
Which decision?

GIS organizes the evidence. Transportation explains movement. Operations research structures choices under constraints.

GIS · Represent the system
Locate stations and demand, inspect patterns, and define the study boundary.
Transportation · Represent access
Distinguish straight line distance from travel over roads. Speed, connectivity and congestion affect reachable areas.
OR · Compare decisions
Use objectives such as covering demand or assigning territories. Check sensitivity before interpreting a result.
